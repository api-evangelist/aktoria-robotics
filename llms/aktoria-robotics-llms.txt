# Aktoria Robotics

> Aktoria Robotics is a San Francisco-based robotics company (Y Combinator Summer 2026 batch) building a teleoperation and remote-operation layer for robots — "Human as API for Robots." Its platform lets operators run any robot anywhere in the world: remotely collecting operational data from robot fleets around the clock, monitoring and intervening in deployed fleets, and moving hardware through a train → deploy → monitor lifecycle. The company is at a pre-product-launch stage and does not yet publish a public developer API, SDKs, or documentation.

## Products

- Data collection: collect data on your robots, remotely, 24 hours a day.
- Fleet management: monitor and intervene in deployed robot fleets.
- Deployment: train → deploy → monitor lifecycle for robots.
